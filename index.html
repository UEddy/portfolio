Intatto

The agent's key is public. Take the money if you can.

A NEAR vault that gives an AI agent its own trading account. The agent trades through Rhea and NEAR Intents within limits the contract enforces, and it can never withdraw. Intent swaps are signed by the NEAR MPC network for the vault, and each AI decision's signed receipt sits in the trade's own transaction.

Obol

Safe invoicing from chat. Forward a client's message, approve the draft, get paid. Every invoice needs your approval, and Obol flags lookalike domains, impersonated clients and instructions hidden in forwarded messages.
